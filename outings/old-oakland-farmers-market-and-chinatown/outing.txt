Friday is market day in Old Oakland. It rained until I parked and stopped as I started walking, and the sun came and went for the rest of the morning.

## 8:38 a.m. | 9th Street and Chinatown
The farmers market fills 9th Street between Broadway and Clay with farm tents, wet pavement and piles of produce. Almost everyone shopping and selling was Asian, many in masks, moving fast with carts and bags.

A few blocks away in Chinatown, the market spills onto the sidewalks: produce on cardboard, vendors on folding stools, travel posters in the windows. I showed people their photos. Some smiled and said thank you; older men who didn't speak English nodded and smiled.

One man looked at his photo and said, "Oh, cool, today's my birthday." I wished him a happy birthday. A good morning at the market.
