Oakland Chinatown - Murals, Vendors and the Produce Market

Monday, October 26, 2026 · Nikon D7500, 18-200mm

A Monday morning in Oakland Chinatown, from 8 to about 10. The sun was low and hard, which is what this neighborhood needs: it rakes across the painted shutters and catches faces under the awnings of the produce stands.

I worked the murals first. A Girl with a Pearl Earring painted on a wall, a snarling fox on a roll-down door, then waited for someone to walk past and finish the picture. The sidewalk vendors setting up for the day gave me the rest: a woman in a pink cap selling dried goods under a red heart sign, another behind a table of jewelry.

Around 9:30 I moved over to the Produce District, where the wholesalers run pallets on forklifts until about 12:30. Less color, more work going on. 105 frames in total; these are the ones that held up.
