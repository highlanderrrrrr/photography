Alameda - Park Street, Webster Street and the Hornet

My first outing with the Nikon Z5 II: a waterfront trail, Park Street and Webster Street after the rain, then the pier where the USS Hornet is docked, under a storm sky.

Date
Mon, Nov 16, 2026
Hours
8:37 – 11:43 a.m.
Light
Overcast after rain, sun breaking through
Gear
Nikon Z5 II, Nikon Z 28-75mm f/2.8
Frames
23 photos
Where
Waterfront trail, Park Street, Webster Street and the USS Hornet pier, Alameda

My first outing with the new Z5 II, and it felt like black magic. Focus stayed locked on runners and cyclists while I moved, and some of the camera's own JPEGs look like a fancy LUT was applied. My compositions suffered a bit; I was a kid in a candy store.

8:37 a.m.Trail by the Water

It had been raining, and the sun was just starting to break through the clouds and haze.

9:05 – 9:32 a.m.Park Street

My approach today: stand in a spot I liked, pretend to shoot something else, and catch people as they passed. I said good morning to a couple of them and smiled at most. A few looked at me like I was about to murder them, which made me smile even more.

10:36 – 11:00 a.m.Webster Street

Webster Street was getting ready for the day: sidewalks swept, leaves blown, chairs set out.

11:35 – 11:43 a.m.USS Hornet pier

Last stop was the pier at the old Naval Air Station, where the USS Hornet is docked. The Doolittle Raid left from this pier in April 1942. I didn't go into the museum; I shot the pier, the cranes and the ship while a storm built over San Francisco.
