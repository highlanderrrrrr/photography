Not a planned outing: a Tuesday on College Avenue with my daughter, shooting along the way.

## 12:00 p.m. | College Avenue
It starts under the Rockridge BART station, where College Avenue runs beneath the tracks, then heads north past cafes, restaurants and small shops.

The street is upscale, and the prices match: a tiny box of chocolates was about $30. Still, every shop had several people inside at midday on a Tuesday, and the sidewalks were busy with shoppers carrying bags and groceries all the way up to the Berkeley line.

## 1:09 p.m. | College and Shafter
After lunch (Thai, reasonably priced and very good), back at the corner of College and Shafter by Rockridge Market Hall. The crowd waiting to cross is on the cover.

## 2:06 p.m. | Channing Way, Berkeley
Later, in Berkeley, on Channing Way a couple of blocks from the UC campus. A street like College Avenue deserves a proper walk; I'll be back.
