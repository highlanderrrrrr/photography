Downtown San Mateo - Crosswalks on a Gray Morning

A Wednesday morning downtown with the 35mm and a flat gray sky: no light to work with, only people and timing.

Date
Wed, Oct 21, 2026
Gear
Nikon D7500, 35mm f/1.8
Frames
2 photos

A Wednesday morning in downtown San Mateo with the 35mm prime. The sky stayed flat gray the whole time, so there was no light to work with, only people and timing.

The best frame came from waiting at one crossing: a man pushing a loaded cart through the crosswalk under the San Mateo Dr sign, the don't-walk hand lit behind him. Down the block I tried a tenth of a second on a woman crossing, to blur her into the street.

About 135 frames, 100 kept. Overcast light and busy backgrounds turned most of the rest into pleasant pictures rather than strong ones. Next time: harder light.
