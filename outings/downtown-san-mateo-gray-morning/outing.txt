I went to downtown San Mateo to come back with a few good photos. A Wednesday morning in late October, with the 35mm on the D7500.

## 9:01 a.m. | Third and Fourth Avenue
It stayed overcast and dim the whole morning, and the city was just waking up. Not many people out, so not many subjects, at least none I could find.

So I walked Third and Fourth and the blocks in between, photographing whatever I thought was kind of cool. Mostly a record of what downtown looked like that morning.

My two favorite frames came from sitting still: 15 or 20 minutes on a planter by a crosswalk on San Mateo Drive, maybe longer, shooting whoever crossed. The man with the cart on the cover came from that planter, and so did the Ghost below.
