About

I'm Lee Sagi. I photograph what I run into — backcountry tracks and desert camps, the streets near home, people I know, and a Weimaraner named Ghost. This is a personal archive rather than a commercial portfolio: everything here is here because I liked the frame, not because it's for sale.

Shot on a Nikon D7500. Based in the East Bay, California.
